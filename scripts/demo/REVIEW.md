# Review

- Creative calls: the film is the site's chroma-key world, built from real material only. The trailer gives the
  cuts (exact) and the colours; the curve beat is a move we animated ourselves and Frame Lens measured back from
  the render, because the trailer reading's 26.04 s "move" turned out to be a cut, a fade and a logo scale-in.
  Hard cuts between beats on purpose: they are what the film measures.
- Measured: `hyperframes check` (no errors), review sheets at 1920 and 360 px, the final file's loudness
  (-19.9 LUFS, true peak -0.5 dBTP) and hit times (each tick peaks one frame after its picture), and Frame Lens's
  own reading of the render (CHECK.md). The sound was measured, not heard.
- Warnings kept: `line_unread` on the opening lead line (2.0 s for 11 words; the head is the read) and on the two
  code lines (1.5 s; their values were already read in the proof scene).
- Weakest shot: the code beat, a short read of two exported lines. With more time: one longer beat that shows the
  exported CSS replaying the measured ease-out next to the original square.
